# Changelog

## v1.6
- First GitHub release.
- **Claude key** button: add your own Claude API key (and pick a model) to use Solve ▸ and Next step outside the hosted preview. The key stays in your browser. Solve offline needs no key.
- Header comment with licence and usage notes.

## v1.5
- The "Solved in …" time now sits at the top right, level with the implicit differentiation checkbox. Emoji removed.

## v1.4
- Solve offline reports how long it took.

## v1.3
- Improper integrals: `inf` and `-inf` bounds and endpoint singularities, with divergence detection.

## v1.2
- Solve offline gives exact radical roots for cubics and quartics (Cardano, Ferrari, rational quadratic factors, biquadratics).

## v1.1
- Trig shorthand such as `cos^2 x` and `sin 2x` is understood.
- Solve offline knows common trig identities.

## v1.0
- First labelled version: step checker for algebra, derivatives, integrals, limits, implicit differentiation and complex numbers; explanations of common mistakes; typeset maths; syntax guide; Solve ▸ and Next step (Claude); Solve offline; LaTeX export.
